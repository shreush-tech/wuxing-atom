# v0.98 — Refined elements + independent workspace

## Goals
- Preserve v0.96/v0.97 performance baseline.
- Restore pleasant inertia without uncontrolled acceleration.
- Make the Five Elements unmistakably clickable.
- Replace blocky hover halo with a smooth round aura.
- Give each lightweight gem a more deliberate polished-jewel material.
- Add the Chinese movement glyph directly to each element with a cheap 2D sprite.
- Thicken golden orbital lines and add visible traveling light packets to suggest energy flow.
- Make desktop left rail and right clinical workspace scroll independently; the 3D stage remains fixed.
- Stop session/pain information from visually overlapping the anamnesis.
- Increase clinical workspace and longitudinal chart typography.

## Performance choices
- No transmission on the five outer gems.
- Clearcoat only on five low-count physical materials.
- Smooth aura uses one modest sphere per element.
- 10 energy movers are one InstancedMesh.
- Four orbital curves retained; line thickness comes from tube radius rather than postprocessing bloom.
- Yin–Yang remains the premium glass focal object.
