# Wu Xing Atom v0.99 — Celestial Interaction

Visual/runtime refinement over v0.98.

- Fixed dark/star-field visual direction; removed background-theme controls from the product surface.
- Five elements changed from faceted placeholder gems to lightweight textured spherical planet/stone forms.
- Kept Chinese movement glyphs as inexpensive 2D sprites.
- Added restrained moving highlight and round golden hover aura to reinforce clickability.
- Golden energy orbits are thicker and now include a bright white inner core plus moving energy packets.
- Element education and Yin–Yang education moved from the anamnesis column into stage-centered modal cards.
- Stage cards close by clicking outside; they no longer permanently occupy the right clinical column.
- Added voice symptom search using the browser SpeechRecognition API when available. Spoken text becomes search text; the user still confirms symptoms before they affect the map.
- No LLM or autonomous clinical inference was added to the voice feature; clinical scoring remains deterministic.
- Preserves the v0.96+ performance strategy: modest geometry, no full-screen post-processing, low DPR, inexpensive procedural canvas textures.
