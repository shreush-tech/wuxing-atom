# v0.91 — Hidden Knowledge

## Fundamental rule
Patterns remain atomic. Physiopathological relationships never manufacture a new pattern. Every imbalance requires its own symptom evidence.

The internal graph may explain Kidney Yin/Yang as roots, failure of Yin to restrain Yang/Heat, and Heat/Fire consuming Yin/Blood, but those relationships do not add or subtract diagnostic scores.

## Knowledge portal
A discreet golden star opens a drawer with:
Wu Xing · Wood · Fire · Earth · Metal · Water · Yin–Yang · Qi · Tao.

Each Five Movement page contains organ pair, season, direction, color, emotion, spirit, taste, sound, classical musical tone, climate, sensory opening, tissue, channel-hour ranges, symbolic life stage and explanatory prose.

Life-stage associations are explicitly labeled symbolic. Qi is presented as a traditional concept, not a measurable physical energy. Golden orbital lines are described as a visual metaphor.

Sources remain internally tracked in `knowledgeProvenance.v091.json`; citations are not displayed in the app.
